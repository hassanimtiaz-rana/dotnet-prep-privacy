Google Play updates and links

Its documentation states that this data is encrypted, is not transferred to third parties, and is deleted after a fixed retention period. Google Play applies its own policies. Do not interpret on-device learning records as a promise of no network activity. Opening reference links connects to the selected website, which applies its own policies.

Deletion and retention

Learning records remain until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. Android backup is disabled for this app’s configuration. Clear site data to remove browser-preview records. Removing those records resets learning progress. The app does not provide cloud synchronization or developer recovery of deleted progress. Update-prompt cooldown preferences are stored in the app’s private Android storage.

Sharing, permissions, and security

We do not sell learning records or send them to an advertising or analytics provider. This app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or location access for learning. Device access controls help protect stored records, but no security guarantee is absolute. Google Play and websites you open process information independently of the developer.
